# Product context

register: product

This starter powers customer-branded Bold video portals. Viewers watch lessons, browse playlists, and ask a video-aware assistant for explanations and timestamped references. Portal owners control account capabilities in Bold.

The interface should let viewers switch between watching, typing, and speaking without losing their place or their draft. Use direct, short labels and calm status feedback. Preserve each tenant's theme and assistant identity. The HRTU reference places a small microphone control beside the send button; voice remains part of the existing conversation.

Avoid a separate call screen, decorative dashboards, or animated effects that compete with the lesson. Access, microphone permission, and session errors must leave ordinary text chat usable. Keyboard access, touch targets, and reduced motion are first-class requirements.
